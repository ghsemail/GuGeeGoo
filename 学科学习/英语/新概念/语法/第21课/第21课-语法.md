# 第 21 课 · Which book? · 语法

> 来源：课程资料 · Lesson 21

---

动词的双宾语：

buy me a book=buy a book for me

Give me a book.=Give a book to me.

双宾语中通常物是直接宾语，人是间接宾语

[课文]

MAN:  	Give me a book please, Jane.

WOMAN:	Which book? This one?

MAN:    	No, not that one.

The red one.

WOMAN:	This one?

MAN:  	Yes, please.

WOMAN:	Here you are.

MAN:   	Thank you.
