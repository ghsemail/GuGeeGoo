# Unit 1 · Day 2 巩固练习

> 顾景源 · 五年级上英语 · 针对 Day 1 错题专项巩固  
> **针对薄弱点：** WP-001 复数 · WP-002 a lot of · WP-003 shouldn't · WP-004 take/eat too much

---

## 一、改错题（每句有一处错误，请写出正确句子）

1. There are five food group on our plate.
2. Eat fruit and vegetable every day.
3. Rice gives us allot of energy.
4. You should eat candy every day.（用 shouldn't 改写）
5. Don't eat too much!（情景：别拿太多食物）→ 改用 take

**参考答案：**

1. There are five food group**s** on our plate.
2. Eat fruit and vegetable**s** every day.
3. Rice gives us **a lot of** energy.
4. You **shouldn't** eat candy every day.
5. Don't **take** too much!

---

## 二、短语填空（根据中文提示，填入英文）

1. We can put food into five __________（食物类别）.
2. __________（水果和蔬菜） are very important.
3. Milk gives us __________（大量的） energy.
4. We should have __________（健康饮食） every day.
5. Potatoes and carrots are in the __________（蔬菜类） group.

**参考答案：**

1. food groups
2. Fruit and vegetables（或 Vegetables and fruit）
3. a lot of
4. healthy eating
5. vegetable

---

## 三、语法填空（should / shouldn't / Don't）

1. You __________ eat too fast.（不应该）
2. We __________ eat slowly.（应该）
3. __________ take too much!（别拿太多！）
4. You __________ have some vegetables.（应该）
5. You __________ eat too much candy.（不应该）

**参考答案：**

1. shouldn't
2. should
3. Don't
4. should
5. shouldn't

---

## 四、选词填空（每空一词，有的需要变形）

**词库：** take · eat · groups · vegetables · a lot of · shouldn't

1. There are five food __________.
2. Apples and bananas are in the fruit __________.
3. Don't __________ too much candy!
4. You __________ eat too fast.
5. __________ fruit and __________ to stay healthy.
6. Rice and bread give us __________ energy.

**参考答案：**

1. groups
2. groups
3. eat
4. shouldn't
5. Eat · vegetables
6. a lot of

---

## 五、情景选择（选 take too much 或 eat too much）

1. 在自助餐，妈妈对你说：「别拿太多！」→ __________
2. 吃糖果太多对身体不好：「不要吃太多糖果！」→ __________
3. Don't __________! We're going to the buffet.（自助餐）
4. Don't __________ candy every day.

**选项：** A. take too much　B. eat too much

**参考答案：**

1. A（take too much）
2. B（eat too much）
3. A
4. B
