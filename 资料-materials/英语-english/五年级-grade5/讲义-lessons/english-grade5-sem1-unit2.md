# Unit 2: A green life

> 顾景源 · 五年级上 · 英语 · 第二单元知识点

---

## 课文 · Get Ready

Everyone can care for the earth.
Let's start today.

Use less waste.
Bring your own bags to the shop.
Don't use too many plastic bags.
Write and draw on both sides of the paper.
And remember, try to eat all your food.
Don't waste it.

Use things again.
Before you throw something away, ask yourself, can I use this again?
For example, you can use an old toothbrush to clean small places.
You can give old toys or clothes to children in need.
Give old things a new life.

Don't throw away cans, glass bottles or old paper.
Put them in the recycling boxes.
They can start a new life.
People use cans to make car parts, use glass bottles to create new windows, and use old paper to make new books.

What can you do to help the earth today?

### 课文结构（便于出题）

| 段落 | 主题 | 要点 |
|------|------|------|
| 开头 | 号召 | care for the earth · Let's start today |
| 减废 | Use less waste | own bags · 少用 plastic bags · 纸两面用 · 不浪费食物 |
| 再用 | Use things again | throw away 前先想 · 旧牙刷 · 旧玩具/衣服给 children in need |
| 回收 | Recycling | cans / glass bottles / old paper → recycling boxes · 变 car parts / windows / books |
| 结尾 | 问句 | What can you do to help the earth today? |

## 重点语法

### better（good 的比较级）

**用法：** `better` 是 `good` 的比较级，表示「更好的」。

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Now they have a better home! | 现在它们有了一个更好的家！ |

### 祈使句（Imperatives）

**用法：** 表示命令、请求、建议、劝告、禁止等。

| 形式 | 结构 |
|------|------|
| 肯定 | **动词原形** + 其他 |
| 否定 | **Don't** + 动词原形 + 其他 |

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Don't throw that old shirt away. Give it to me, please. | 别扔掉那件旧衬衫。请把它给我。 |

---

## 核心句型

### 1. 用祈使句表达「做某事」

**句型：** 动词原形 + 其他

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Take it. | 拿去吧。 |

### 2. 用祈使句表达「不要做某事」

**句型：** `Don't` + 动词原形 + 其他

| 例句 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| Don't throw it away. | 不要扔掉它。 |

### 3. 对话示例（课文/练习常见）

| 英文 | 中文 |
|------|------|
| — Don't throw it away. | 不要扔掉它。 |
| — Take it. But what for? | 拿去吧。但用来做什么呢？ |
| — Wait and see! | 等等看！ |

## 单元主题小结

本单元围绕 **环保生活（A green life）** 展开，重点掌握：

- 减废、再用、回收（Reduce · Reuse · Recycle）相关词汇
- `throw away`、`turn into`、`take off`、`live a green life` 等短语
- **祈使句**肯定 / 否定（与 Unit 1 的 `Don't ...` 建议一脉相承，本单元更侧重环保行动）
- `use ... to ...`、`give ... to ...` 等表达用途与给予
