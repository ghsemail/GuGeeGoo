### B. 语法练习

| 题号 | 答案 | 说明 |
|------|------|------|
| 16 | **A. should** | 建议每天吃果蔬，用 should |
| 17 | **B. too many** | 吃 chips 用 eat；可数名词用 too many |
| 18 | **groups** | five 后名词用复数 food groups |
| 19 | **a lot of** | 三词短语「大量的」，勿写成 allot（分配） |
| 20 | **shouldn't** | shouldn't = should not，撇号在 n 前 |
| 21 | **eat** | 吃糖果用 eat；take 多用于自助餐「拿取」 |
| 22 | **vegetables** | 与 fruit 并列时常用复数 vegetables |
| 23 | **You shouldn't eat candy every day.** | 正确缩写为 shouldn't，不是 should'nt |
| 24 | **B. slowly** | slow 是形容词，slowly 是副词；修饰 eat 用 slowly |
| 25 | 示例：**Don't skip breakfast. We should eat breakfast every day.** | 两句结构对应例句：Don't… / We should… |
| 26 | **Candy** | 糖果对牙齿不好；句首大写 Candy |
