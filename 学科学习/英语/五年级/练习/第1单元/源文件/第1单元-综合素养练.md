## 十一、阅读理解 · 书信（10 分）

*阅读 Rocky 给 Tiantian 的信，选择最佳答案。*

Dear Tiantian,

I'm your family doctor. We know everyone wants to be healthy. How can you keep healthy? Here is some advice for you.

① Please eat a healthy breakfast. It is very good for your health. It's a good beginning of the day.

② Please go to bed early and get up early. If you sleep well, you'll have energy to read books, play sports and do what you want to do.

③ Please eat some fruit like apples, oranges, bananas and so on. Vegetables can make you healthy, too.

④ Please don't eat junk food, because it's really bad!

Do these above, and you'll be healthy.

Rocky
