## 听力材料（供家长核对）

**一、**

1. I have some bread and milk for breakfast.
2. Just a few biscuits are not enough.
3. You should have some vegetables, like tomatoes.
4. Don't take too many candies. You can take an apple.
5. You should have some vegetables, like potatoes.

**二、**

1. What do you want to eat?
2. Thank you for your advice.
3. What should I have?
4. What snacks do you eat?
5. What do you know about food groups?

**三、**（Diandian 与 Tom）

Diandian: Hello, Tom. You look tired. What's wrong?

Tom: I have a lot of meat and cola. No vegetables.

Diandian: That's not good. You should eat more vegetables and fruit. And don't drink too much cola.

Tom: What about breakfast? I have only a biscuit.

Diandian: You need a good breakfast—like bread, milk and an egg. It helps you stay active.

Tom: OK. I will try to change.

**四、**（Diandian 自述）

Hello, I'm Diandian. For breakfast, I have bread and milk. They are healthy. For lunch, I have noodles with vegetables and a little beef. I don't drink cola. I think it is bad. For dinner, I often have apples. They are good for me.

## 十三、书面表达 · 范文

**My Healthy Diet**

Hello, I'm Diandian. For breakfast, I have bread and milk. They are healthy. For lunch, I have noodles with vegetables and a little beef. For dinner, I often have soup and some fruit. I don't drink cola. These good habits help me stay healthy.
